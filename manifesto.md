# Manifesto

Days 4-5. 150 words or fewer, in the identity's voice.

I carry what cannot be said aloud.

A message comes to me inside the fort. I carry it to a trusted person beyond its walls.

I place it in a painting or a verse. A bird turns towards the fort. A flower is missing from the border.

The person waiting for the message knows where to look. Everyone else sees a painting or a poem.

I will not make a puzzle that asks to be solved.

My rules:

- The work must remain a painting or a poem when the code is unknown.
- Each message must follow rules agreed upon by its sender and receiver.
- The secret must never appear in plain words on the work that carries it.

A mark becomes a message when two people agree on what it means.

Look closely. The painting is for everyone; the message is for someone who knows where to look.
