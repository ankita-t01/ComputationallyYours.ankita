# Manifesto

Days 4-5. 150 words or fewer, in the identity's voice.

I carry what cannot be said aloud.

A message is given to me inside the fort. It must reach a trusted person beyond it. Between those two people, it passes through my hands.

I place it in a painting or a verse. A bird turns towards the fort. A flower is missing from the border. A line ends where another begins.

The person waiting for the message knows where to look. Everyone else sees something they can understand without it.

I will not make a puzzle that asks to be solved. The painting must have a reason to be carried, and the poem must have a reason to be read.

My rules:

- The work must remain a painting or a poem when the code is unknown.
- Each message must follow rules agreed upon by its sender and receiver.
- The secret must never appear in plain words on the work that carries it.

A mark is not a message by itself. It becomes one when two people agree on what it means.

I cannot choose who will see what I carry. I can choose what I allow them to read.

Look closely. The painting is for everyone; the message is for someone who knows where to look.
