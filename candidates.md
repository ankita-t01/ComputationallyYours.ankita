# Three candidate identities

Day 2. One paragraph each, each tied to a real condition.

## 1 · Maya, messenger in verse

Condition: the need to carry secrets across the political conflicts of seventeenth-century Maharashtra. Maya is a spy from Raigad who delivers coded messages through artwork and poetry. A painted detail or a carefully chosen line can carry news that would be dangerous to say aloud. Evidence to hand: miniature paintings, handwritten verses, and letters whose meanings change depending on who reads them.

## 2 · Reverie, collector of dreams

Condition: the way dreams travel through memory, culture, and everyday life, then disappear upon waking. Reverie is a dream researcher in present-day Lyon who documents dreams shared by people around the world and records them in her journals. As her collection grows, recurring images begin to connect strangers across places and languages. Evidence to hand: interview notes, dream journals, recordings, and sketches made from descriptions.

## 3 · Eden, designer of lost flowers

Condition: the extinction of floral species and the loss of the habitats that once sustained them. Eden is a designer in a future Tokyo who attempts to recreate extinct flowers from fragments of evidence. Each reconstruction is both an imagined bloom and a record of what remains unknown about it. Evidence to hand: botanical archives, preserved specimens, old photographs, and digital models.
